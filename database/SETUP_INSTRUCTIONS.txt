ENERGY BUSINESS LOGIC:
=================================================================

1. EARN ENERGY:
   - Order Completion: 1 point per Rp 1,000 spent (minimum 10 pts)
     Example: Order Rp 25,000 = 25 Energy points
   - QR Scan: Amount set by admin (default 10 pts)
   - Admin Adjustment: Manual credit/debit by admin

2. REDEEM ENERGY:
   - Discount Rp 5k = 50 pts
   - Discount Rp 10k = 100 pts
   - Free Americano = 150 pts
   - Free Kopi Jago = 200 pts
   - Tumbler = 500 pts
   - T-Shirt = 800 pts

3. QR SCAN RULES:
   - One QR code per customer (can't double scan)
   - QR expires after set duration (default 24h)
   - Scan limit enforced (e.g., first 50 customers)
   - Invalid/expired QR → error message

4. REDEMPTION:
   - Deducts Energy immediately
   - Creates redemption record (status: approved)
   - Expires after 30 days if not claimed
   - Stock checked (if limited stock reward)
   - Notification sent to customer
